A new puzzle opens in the last mode you used for that crossword, or in the mode set in Settings. You can switch mode until the puzzle is finished. Until you use a check, hint or reveal, a solve counts at the mode you finish in; after that it counts at the easiest mode used. Once you have used a hint or reveal, Hard and Champ are locked for that puzzle. Clearing the grid removes only the letters: the time, mode and any reveals stay.
